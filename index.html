FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Simple answers to what most teams ask before joining Auditor.

How fast can we get started?+
Right away, with the ready-made templates: Building Condition Assessment, Home Inspection, Accessibility Assessment and Construction Inspection. If you choose our template customization service to digitize your own workflows, setup usually takes a few weeks depending on complexity.
Is there a free plan?+
The desktop app is a professional tool with no free tier, but every plan starts with a 2-week free trial and we're happy to give you a personalized demo first. Site Capture is free to download; your own templates and report branding need a licence or subscription.
Can we integrate with our existing tools?+
Yes. Auditor fits into your existing reporting process: field data, photos and calculations export straight into formatted Word documents and Excel spreadsheets.
How secure is our data?+
Data security is a top priority. Your assessment data, custom formulas and user credentials are encrypted, so your workflows, client data and building information stay confidential.
Do you support enterprise teams?+
Absolutely. Enterprise plans include priority support, bespoke integrations and unlimited seats for large portfolios.
Can we customize the platform?+
Customization is our core strength. Through our one-time template implementation service we recreate your existing spreadsheets, conditional logic and branded Word deliverables inside Auditor.
How do payments work?+
Licences are billed as a subscription, monthly or yearly, in Canadian dollars plus applicable tax. Template customization is billed separately as a one-time setup fee.
Where is your team based?+
In Ontario, Canada. We understand the demands of local standards like the OBC and AODA, and build Auditor to handle international frameworks like ASTM too.
Do I need the desktop app to use Site Capture?+
No. Build a template from a three-tab Excel file, capture on site and export on your own. The desktop app turns the captured data into a finished report.
I already subscribe to Auditor. Do I pay again for Site Capture?+
No. Your Auditor purchase includes a Site Capture licence key. Enter it in Site Capture under Settings → Your version.
Does Site Capture work without a signal?+
Yes. Capture and export work offline. Only Asset Match and Google Lens need a connection.
Is there an iPhone version?+
Yes. On iPhone and iPad, open sitecapture.theauditorapp.com in Safari and add it to your Home Screen. On Android, get it from Google Play.
